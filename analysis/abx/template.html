{{PLUG}} Blind Listening

A is the {{UNIT}}, B is the plug-in. X is one of them at random. Switch freely while it plays (the switch keeps the position), then say which one X is, or that you can't tell. Ten trials per program; eight or more right means you reliably hear a difference. "Can't tell" is a useful answer: it counts as not right, and the saved result records how often you chose it.
